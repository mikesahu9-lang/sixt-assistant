Rental Information
In General
A rental day consists of each consecutive 24-hour period beginning after the start time of the rental (regardless of time zones and daylight-saving times effects).

A reservation can be made for up to 365 days. Reservations of over 28 days will be processed in 28-day increments until the entire length of the reservation is fulfilled. The first rental agreement will be provided to the renter at pick-up. Subsequent rental agreements will be created remotely and sent to the customer via email. The renter must review, electronically sign, and provide the vehicle's current mileage for each subsequent rental agreement.

To extend an active rental, please visit sixt.com/service or call SIXT at +1 888 SIXT CAR (749 8227). Rentals can be extended until the 28th day of the rental, as long as the following conditions are met:
- The Renter's Driver's license is valid throughout the new rental period.
- The incremental authorization on the payment method on file is successfully processed.
- The vehicle selected has enough remaining days and mileage to fulfill the new rental period.
Any extension past the 28th day of the current rental agreement must be processed as a new rental agreement. Please be advised any rentals occurring in the following locations do not offer rental extensions over the phone and require the customer to come into the branch in-person if they wish to extend: all non-airport locations in Texas and Illinois.

Driver's License Requirements
The renter and all authorized drivers must present a valid, unexpired and original driver's license at the time of the rental. The driver's license must be legible, in good condition, and valid for the entire rental period.
Temporary licenses are only accepted when presented alongside the original expired or voided driver's license. Digital licenses, photocopies and Learner's Permits are not accepted.

Driver's licenses that restrict the driver to the use and operation of a vehicle equipped with a form of a breathalyzer apparatus are not accepted.
For Florida only: According to state law, certain, limited out-of-state driver's licenses with the following indications "DO - Not for Federal Identification" and "Driving Privilege Only - Not Valid for Identification" are not recognized by the State of Florida to drive a vehicle on public roads, and thus, will not be accepted.
Renters with a non-U.S. driver's license may be required to present a valid passport or other government-issued ID as a second form of identification.

Driver's licenses printed in a non-Roman alphabet (Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Cyrillic, etc.) must be complemented by an international driver's license. For driver's licenses from countries not part of the international driver's license treaty an official translation is required.
Sixt supports Chinese customers with the necessary translations via Sixt WeChat Account or via following link: Link . This or any other kind of valid translation of the driving license can only be accepted if accompanied by the original driving license.

Extended Roadside Protection
SIXT will provide any renter who has purchased the optional Extended Roadside Protection coverage with 24/7 breakdown assistance (where available) without any additional charge. It protects the renter at home and abroad against high service and repair costs for the following incidents:

1) Key lockout service: If a renter experiences locking of the key or key fob inside the vehicle during their rental period, SIXT will cover the cost of a lockout service, by either the vehicle manufacturer or a 3rd party service provider, cost of delivering a new vehicle or towing of the vehicle to the nearest SIXT location.

2) Lost key or key fob: If a renter experiences a lost or misplaced key or key fob during their rental period, SIXT will cover the cost of replacing the key or key fob, the cost of delivering a new set of keys (if available), towing of the vehicle to the nearest Sixt location and for a replacement vehicle.

3) Flat tire service: If a renter experiences a flat tire during the rental period, SIXT will cover the cost of assisting to replace the tire either by a 3rd party service provider, delivering a new vehicle, or another solution with the goal of handling the roadside assistance. Damages caused to the tire will not be covered without the purchase of Loss Damage Waiver.

4) Dead Battery: If a renter experiences a situation during their rental period, SIXT will cover the cost of jumpstart service, by either the vehicle manufacturer or a 3rd party service provider, the cost of delivering a new vehicle or towing of the vehicle to the nearest SIXT location.

5) Towing: If it is not possible to move the vehicle (e.g., due to wintery road conditions, stuck in the mud, underestimated road conditions, or incidents not being repairable on site) SIXT will cover the costs for the vehicle to be towed away (to the nearest SIXT location) and for a replacement vehicle. Incidents not being repairable on site, like mechanical failures aside from being a result of a collision, are covered by our SIXT basic standards. Damages caused by the collision will not be covered, please refer to "Loss Damage Waiver".

6) Breakdown caused by empty fuel tank: If a renter experiences a breakdown due to running out of fuel, SIXT will cover the costs for breakdown assistance and fuel required to continue your trip.

When deciding whether to purchase Extended Roadside Protection, it is recommended renters check whether they have this coverage available through their personal auto insurance carrier or through a 3rd party carrier. Extended Roadside Protection is not insurance.

All services may only be ordered by a direct claim with SIXT 24-hour Extended Roadside Protection. SIXT will determine the type and extent of service required to ensure the renter stays mobile. If any damage to the rental vehicle occurs which is not covered by SIXT within the scope of the Extended Roadside Protection, the renter is liable for such damage.
